# Frases comunes para Morning Conversations
## Phrases
### Rise and shine
Definition. To wake someone up.
### My alarm **went off** at 7:00, but I just **hit snooze**
#### Go off
Definition. An alarm makes noise.
#### Snooze
Definition. An informal way to say "sleep".
### How did you sleep?
### I didn't sleep well. I was **tossing and turning** all night
Definition. You keep moving, you can't get comfortable in bed.
### Any plans for the day?
Any plans for...?
### We need to head out the door in five minutes
Definition. Leave the house.
### Before I head out the door, I always need a **cup of Joe**
Definition. A cup of coffee.
### She got up on the wrong side of the bed
## Examples
### First example
I suddenly woke up when I heard the smoke detector **going off**. Panicked, I jumped out of bed and ran intho the kitchen. My dad was standing in our smoke-filled kitchen with a big smile on his face.
"**Rise and shine**," he said cheerfully. "Want some burnt toast?"
"How on earth did you burn your toast?" I asked angrily. "You've made like 1,000 slices in your life. Do you hav any idea what time it is?"
"Well, it seems like someone **got up on the wrong side of the bed**," my dad replied.
"Open the window and help me fan some of this smoke out."
...
"I'm going back to bed. See you in a few hours. Don't burn the house down."
"But it's already 6! Have **a cup of Joe** with me!".
I finally agreed to sit with my dad. He asked me the usual question like, "**any plans for the day**? **How did you sleep**?"
I explained that I didn't sleep well. I was **tossing and turning** all night.
"Well, since you're up," he said, "want to join me for a morning run? I'm going to **head out** in a few minutes."
