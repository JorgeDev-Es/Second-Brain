Creational patterns (like Factory, Singleton, Builder) are about how objects are created.
They abstract the instantiation process.
Structural patterns (like Adapter, Decorator, Composite) are about how classes and objects are composed to form larger structures. They deal with the relationships and arrangements of classes and objects.
Behavioral patterns are about the dynamic interaction and communication between objects. They identify common patterns of communication and collaboration, making these interactions mor flexible, efficient, and maintainable.
