# Simple Present

The simple present tense is used to describe habitual actions, routines, or general truths.
> "I go to the gym"
> In this case, the speaker is describing a regular habit of going to the gym every morning before work.

- The simple present version (**"I go"**) focuses on the **regularity** of the action as a general truth or habit.
    
- The present perfect version (**"I have gone"**) emphasizes that this habit has been **ongoing up to now**, and it might imply that you are reflecting on this habit in relation to the present moment. For example, you might be saying this to highlight that you've been consistent with your gym routine recently.
