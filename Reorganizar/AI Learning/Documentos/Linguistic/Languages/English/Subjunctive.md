The **subjunctive** is a grammatical mood used to express **wishes, hypothetical situations, demands, suggestions, or things that are not necessarily real**. It's not very common in everyday English, but it's still important to understand—especially in formal writing and speech.

### Common uses of the subjunctive in English:

1. **Wishes or hypothetical statements:**
    
    - _I wish he **were** here._ (Not “was” – “were” is the subjunctive form.)
        
    - _If I **were** you, I’d study more._
        
2. **Suggestions, demands, or recommendations (often after certain verbs or expressions):**
    
    - _I suggest that she **study** more._ (Not “studies”)
        
    - _It is essential that he **be** on time._ (Not “is”)
        
3. **Fixed expressions:**
    
    - _God **bless** you._
        
    - _Long **live** the king!_
