# Stack
The word **stack** primarily refers to a pile or heap of things arranged neatly, or the act of arranging them in such a way. It also has specialized meanings in computing, aviation, and for large quantities of things.
## Meaning

### A neat pile or heap
This meaning refers to an orderly arrangement of objects placed one on top of another. It's used as a noun.
#### Examples
> "She placed a **stack** of books on the table." "He had a neat **stack** of papers on his desk."
### To arrange in a pile

This usage refers to the action of putting things into a stack. It's used as a verb.
#### Examples
> "Please **stack** the chairs after the meeting." "They were busy **stacking** shelves in the supermarket."
### A large quantity or number
Informally, **stack** can mean a large amount of something. It's used as a noun.

#### Examples

> "We've got **stacks** of work to do." "She has **stacks** of money after her successful business venture."

### In computing: A data structure
In computer science, a **stack** is a data structure for temporary storage where the last item stored is the first one retrieved (LIFO - Last In, First Out). It's used as a noun.
#### Examples

> "The program uses a **stack** to manage function calls." "Pushing data onto the **stack** adds it to the top."
### In aviation: Aircraft waiting to land
This refers to a group of aircraft circling over an airport at different altitudes, waiting for permission to land. It's used as a noun.

#### Examples

> "We were in the **stack** for over an hour due to heavy air traffic." "The air traffic controller told the pilot to join the **stack**."
### To arrange unfairly for a desired result
This usage, often in the idiom "stack the deck," means to manipulate or arrange things secretly or unfairly to achieve a particular outcome. It's used as a verb.
#### Examples
> "The other players accused him of **stacking** the deck." "The rules were **stacked** against us from the start."
### A tall chimney or flue
This meaning refers to a vertical pipe or structure, especially one carrying off smoke from a factory or ship (also known as a smokestack). It's used as a noun.
#### Examples
> "Smoke billowed from the factory **stack**." "The ship's **stack** emitted thick black smoke."
### Library shelves

In libraries, "the stacks" refers to the areas with rows of bookshelves for compact storage of books. It's used as a plural noun.
#### Examples
> "I found the old manuscript deep within the library **stacks**." "The librarian helped me locate the book in the remote **stacks**."
## Phonetic and pronunciation

The pronunciation of **stack** is generally consistent in both its strong and weak forms, as it's a short, monosyllabic word.

- **Pronunciation:** /stæk/ (American English and British English)
- **Sounds:**     - The "s" sound is unvoiced, as in "snake."     - The "t" sound is unvoiced, as in "top."     - The "a" sound is a short "a" sound, as in "cat" or "trap."     - The "ck" sound is a hard "k" sound, as in "kick."

There isn't a significantly different "weak form" for **stack** in connected speech, as its core sounds are already quite concise. It maintains its /stæk/ pronunciation.

## Synonyms

### Synonyms of "Stack" (as a Noun - Pile/Heap)

- **Pile:** This is a very common synonym, often interchangeable, though "stack" can imply more neatness.     - _Example:_ A **pile** of clothes.
- **Heap:** Similar to pile, but can sometimes suggest a less organized or haphazard collection.     - _Example:_ A **heap** of rubble.
- **Mound:** A rounded pile, often of earth or stones.     - _Example:_ A **mound** of dirt.
- **Bundle:** A collection of things tied or wrapped together, but can also imply a neat collection.     - _Example:_ A **bundle** of newspapers.

### Synonyms of "Stack" (as a Noun - Large Quantity)

- **Lot:** A very common informal synonym for a large quantity.     - _Example:_ A **lot** of money.
- **Mass:** A large body or quantity of something.     - _Example:_ A **mass** of evidence.
- **Plenty:** A sufficient or more than sufficient amount.     - _Example:_ We have **plenty** of time.
- **Slew:** (Informal) A large number or quantity.     - _Example:_ A **slew** of problems.

### Synonyms of "Stack" (as a Verb - To pile)

- **Pile (up):** To arrange things in a pile.     - _Example:_ He began to **pile** up the dirty dishes.
- **Heap (up):** To put things into a heap.     - _Example:_ She **heaped** the vegetables onto her plate.
- **Arrange:** To put things in a particular order or position.     - _Example:_ He **arranged** the books on the shelf.
- **Stow:** To pack or store something tidily.     - _Example:_ They **stowed** their luggage in the overhead compartment.
