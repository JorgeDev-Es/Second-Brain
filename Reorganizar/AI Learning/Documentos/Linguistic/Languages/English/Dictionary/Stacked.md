# Stacked
The word **stacked** is an adjective that, when referring to a person, primarily describes someone, typically a woman, with a curvaceous figure, especially a large bust. It can also, less commonly, imply a person who is very muscular or well-built.
## Meaning
### Curvaceous or well-built (especially a large bust)
When referring to a person, particularly a woman, **stacked** means having a very full, curvaceous figure, especially in the chest area. Informally, it can also describe someone, male or female, who is heavily muscled or powerfully built. It's used as an adjective.
#### Examples
> "She's got a really **stacked** figure." (referring to a woman's curvaceous body) "The bodybuilder was incredibly **stacked**." (referring to a person with a lot of muscle)
## Phonetic and pronunciation
The pronunciation of **stacked** is consistent.
- **Pronunciation:** /stækt/ (American English and British English)
- **Sounds:**     - The "s" sound is unvoiced, as in "snake."     - The "t" sound is unvoiced, as in "top."     - The "a" sound is a short "a" sound, as in "cat" or "trap."     - The "ck" sound is a hard "k" sound, as in "kick."     - The "ed" at the end makes a /t/ sound, as it follows an unvoiced consonant sound (/k/).
There is no distinct "weak form" for **stacked**; it maintains its /stækt/ pronunciation in all contexts.
## Synonyms
### Synonyms of "Stacked" (referring to a person's physique)
- **Busty:** (Specifically for women) Having a large bust.     - _Example:_ A **busty** woman.
- **Curvy:** Having an attractive, rounded figure.     - _Example:_ A **curvy** silhouette.
- **Voluptuous:** Characterized by a full, generous, and attractive figure.     - _Example:_ A **voluptuous** actress.
- **Well-endowed:** (Often euphemistic) Having a large bust.     - _Example:_ A **well-endowed** model.
- **Proportionate:** While not a direct synonym for "stacked" in the sense of large, it can imply a well-formed figure.
- **Muscular:** (More general, applies to both sexes) Having well-developed muscles.     - _Example:_ A **muscular** athlete.
- **Well-built:** (More general, applies to both sexes) Having a strong and sturdy physique.     - _Example:_ A **well-built** individual.
## Lexical equivalent
The word **stacked** when referring to a person has lexical equivalents in many languages, though the connotations can vary slightly.

The word **stacked** has lexical equivalents like "con curvas pronunciadas," "bien dotada" (for women, often euphemistic), "voluptuosa," or "con buen cuerpo." For a very muscular person, "corpulento" or "musculoso."
### German
The word **stacked** has lexical equivalents like "kurvenreich," "gut gebaut" (can apply to both sexes), "vollbusig" (for women, specifically busty), or "wohlgeformt." For a very muscular person, "muskulös."
### French
The word **stacked** has lexical equivalents like "bien roulée," "pulpeuse," "bien faite" (for women), or "voluptueuse." For a very muscular person, "musclé" or "baraqué" (informal).
### Italian
The word **stacked** has lexical equivalents like "formoso/a," "prosperoso/a" (for women), "curvilineo/a," or "ben messo/a." For a very muscular person, "muscoloso/a."
