# Such
2. Intensifier

## Key differences with other nuances
While "so much", "to such a great extent," and "really/very" can convey a similar idea of intensity, they are **not always interchangeable** with "such" in every construction, and they do have differences in their typical usage and the specific emphasis they provide.
- **Placement and Structure:** "Such" typically precedes "a/an + adjective + noun" (e.g., "such a beautiful day," "such good care"). "Really" and "very" precede just the adjective (e.g., "really beautiful," "very good").
- **Implied Emotion/Emphasis:** "Such" often carries a stronger emotional weight – admiration, surprise, or a sense of "wow!" – that "really" or "very" don't always fully capture.
- **Conciseness:** "Such good care" is more concise and idiomatic than "care to such a great extent" or even "so much good care" in this context.
