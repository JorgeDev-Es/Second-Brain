# Doable
The word **doable** means capable of being done, achieved, or performed; possible or feasible.
## Meaning
### Capable of being done
This usage of **doable** describes something that is possible to accomplish or carry out. It's used as an adjective.
#### Examples
> "The task seems difficult, but I believe it's totally doable with enough effort." "Setting up the new system in a day might be tight, but it's doable."
## Phonetic and pronunciation
The **strong form** of **doable** is used when the word is emphasized or spoken clearly.
- **Pronunciation:** /ˈduː.ə.bəl/ (American English and British English)
- **Sounds:**     - The "doo" sounds like "doo" in "door" but shorter.     - The "a" is a schwa sound, like the "a" in "about."     - The "ble" sounds like "bul" in "bubble."
The **weak form** of **doable** does not have a distinctly different pronunciation from its strong form in everyday speech, as its vowel sounds are already somewhat reduced. However, in rapid, connected speech, the emphasis on each syllable might be slightly less pronounced.
## Synonyms
- **Achievable:** Capable of being brought about or accomplished.     - _Example:_ Our goals are ambitious but **achievable**.
- **Attainable:** Capable of being reached or acquired.     - _Example:_ With hard work, success is **attainable**.
- **Feasible:** Possible to do easily or conveniently.     - _Example:_ The proposed plan is economically **feasible**.
- **Possible:** Capable of happening or existing.     - _Example:_ It's **possible** to finish the project ahead of schedule.
- **Practicable:** Able to be done or put into practice successfully.     - _Example:_ We need a **practicable** solution to this problem.
- **Realizable:** Capable of being actualized or brought into being.     - _Example:_ Her dreams of becoming a doctor are perfectly **realizable**.
- **Viable:** Capable of working successfully; feasible.     - _Example:_ Is this business idea truly **viable**?
- **Workable:** Capable of being put into practice effectively.     - _Example:_ We need to find a **workable** compromise.
## Lexical equivalent
The word **doable** has lexical equivalents in many languages.
