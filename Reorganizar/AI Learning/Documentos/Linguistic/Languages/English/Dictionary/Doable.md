# Doable
## Lexical equivalent
The word **doable** does have lexical equivalents in many languages.
### Spanish
The word **doable** has lexical equivalents like "factible," "realizable," and "posible."
### German
The word **doable** has lexical equivalents like "machbar" and "realisierbar."
### French
The word **doable** has lexical equivalents like "faisable" and "réalisable."
### Italian
The word **doable** has lexical equivalents like "fattibile" and "realizzabile."
