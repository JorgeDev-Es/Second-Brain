# Wörterbuch
The word **Wörterbuch** refers to a book or electronic resource that lists words of a language alphabetically and provides their meanings, definitions, pronunciations, and often their etymology, usage examples, and translations into other languages.
## Meaning
### Dictionary or Wordbook
This usage of **Wörterbuch** means a compendium of words, usually arranged alphabetically, giving information about their meaning, pronunciation, and forms.
#### Examples
> "Ich schlage das Wort im Wörterbuch nach." This means "I look up the word in the dictionary." "Dieses Wörterbuch enthält Beispielsätze." This means "This dictionary has example sentences."
## Phonetic and pronunciation
- **Pronunciation:** /ˈvœrtɐbuːx/
- **Sounds:**
    - The "W" at the beginning sounds like an English "V".
    - "ö" is a rounded front vowel, similar to the "ir" in "bird" in some English accents, but more rounded.
    - "er" sounds like a schwa /ə/ or a short "uh" sound.
    - "t" is pronounced as in English.
    - "er" again as a schwa /ə/.
    - "b" is pronounced as in English.
    - "u" is like the "oo" in "moon."
    - "ch" is a soft, guttural sound, like the "ch" in Scottish "loch" or German "Bach".
## Synonyms
- **Lexikon:** Often used interchangeably with Wörterbuch, though sometimes implying a more encyclopedic content.
- **Vokabularbuch:** More specifically refers to a vocabulary book, especially one for learning.
- **Sprachführer:** A phrasebook, which is a type of specialized dictionary for travelers.
- **Glossar:** A glossary, usually for a specific text or field.
