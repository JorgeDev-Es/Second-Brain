# Aussprache

The word **Aussprache** means "pronunciation" or "discussion."

## Meaning

### The way a word or language is spoken

This usage of **Aussprache** refers to the manner in which sounds are produced when speaking a word, phrase, or language. It encompasses accent, articulation, and the correct formation of sounds.

#### Examples

> "Die korrekte Aussprache eines Wortes ist wichtig." This means "The correct pronunciation of a word is important."

> "Man merkt an seiner Aussprache, dass er Österreicher ist." This means "You can tell by his pronunciation that he is an Austrian."

### A clarifying discussion or talk

When used this way, **Aussprache** refers to a conversation or discussion, often one intended to clarify something, resolve an issue, or exchange views.

#### Examples

> "Wir hatten eine lange Aussprache über das Problem." This means "We had a long discussion about the problem."

> "Eine Aussprache herbeiführen." This means "To initiate a discussion."

## Phonetic and pronunciation

The pronunciation of **Aussprache** is /ˈaʊ̯sˌʃpʁaːχə/.

- **Sounds:**
    - "Au" sounds like "ow" in "cow."
    - "ss" is a sharp "s" sound.
    - "spr" is similar to the "spr" in "spring."
    - "a" is a long "ah" sound.
    - "ch" is a guttural sound, similar to the "ch" in Scottish "loch" or German "Bach."
    - "e" is a short "uh" sound.

## Synonyms

When "Aussprache" means "pronunciation":

- **Artikulation:** (Articulation) Refers to the physical act of producing speech sounds.
- **Lautbildung:** (Sound formation) The process of forming speech sounds.
- **Sprechweise:** (Manner of speaking) The way someone speaks, including their pronunciation and accent.
- **Akzent:** (Accent) A distinctive mode of pronunciation of a language, especially one associated with a particular nation, locality, or social class.

When "Aussprache" means "discussion":

- **Gespräch:** (Conversation, talk) A general term for an exchange of words.
- **Diskussion:** (Discussion) A formal or informal debate or exchange of opinions.
- **Unterredung:** (Interview, talk) A formal or serious conversation.
- **Austausch:** (Exchange) An exchange of views or ideas.
- **Wortwechsel:** (Verbal exchange, argument) An exchange of words, which can sometimes imply a dispute.
