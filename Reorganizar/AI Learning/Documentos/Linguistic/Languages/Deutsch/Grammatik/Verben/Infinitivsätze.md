# Infinitivsätze
Infinitivsatze is a German grammatical term that refers to infinitive clauses-- subordinate clauses that use the infinitive form of a verb, often introduced by "zu" (to).
They function similarly to infinitive phrases in English.
## Structure
1. With "zu" + Infinitive
Used with verbs that require an infinitive construction.
2. Without "zu"
3. Um ... zu + Infinitive
Expresses purpose (Similar to "in order to" in English)
1. Ohne ... zu + Infinitive
2. Anstatt ... zu + Infinitive
