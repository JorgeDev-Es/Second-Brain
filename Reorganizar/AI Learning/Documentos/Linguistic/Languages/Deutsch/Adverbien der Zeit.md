# Adverbien der Zeit
In German, time adverbs (Temporaladverbien) provide information about _when_, _how often_, or _how long_ an action takes place. They are a crucial part of sentence construction and can appear in different positions depending on emphasis and sentence structure.
## Word order
A general rule for German adverbs when multiple types are present is the **Time-Manner-Place (TMP) rule**:

- **Time** adverbs usually come first.
- Then **Manner** adverbs (how something happens).
- Finally, **Place** adverbs (where something happens).

**Examples:**

- **Ich gehe heute ins Kino.** (I am going to the cinema today.) - _heute_ is a time adverb.
- **Wir sehen uns morgen.** (We'll see each other tomorrow.)
- **Er arbeitet montags von zu Hause.** (He works from home on Mondays.)
- **Stephan isst selten Fleisch.** (Stephan seldom eats meat.) - _selten_ is a frequency adverb.
- **Ich habe gestern hart im Fitnessstudio trainiert.** (I trained rigorously at the gym yesterday.) - _gestern_ (time), _hart_ (manner), _im Fitnessstudio_ (place).
